Why the memory claim is checkable, not asserted

1

No quote, no fact

Every stored fact carries a literal span of something the patient actually said, in a turn that was hers. Three conditions, eleven lines, and anything else is dropped.

2

Nothing is deleted

A contradiction retires the old fact; it never removes it. The professional can always see what was believed, when, and what replaced it.

3

The escalation is code

The red-flag guard is 32 deterministic lines with a negation window. The model phrases the escalation; it never decides on one.

Who buys

A physiotherapist, a midwife, a chronic-care nurse — anyone with a caseload of people who should be doing something at home between appointments.

Monthly subscription per professional, tiered by active patients. Synthflow starts at US$99 a month; a lower entry price aimed at Latin America is competitive.

What is actually built

319

tests green with no keys

The whole pipeline runs with no environment variable set and no database. The panel's buttons drive the real orchestrator against a scripted patient.

2

products, three capabilities

Universal-Streaming v3 live over the phone; Speech Understanding on the recording for entities, sentiment and key phrases. Feeding those phrases back to prime the next call was built, measured on a real recording, and reverted.

5

limits we say out loud

No authentication. English only. Facts land seconds after the hangup. Key phrases are shown, not fed back to the recogniser. The agent never diagnoses.
