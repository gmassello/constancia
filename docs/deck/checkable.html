Why the memory claim is checkable, not asserted

1

No quote, no fact

Every stored fact carries a literal span of something the patient actually said, in a turn that was hers. Three conditions, eleven lines, and anything else is dropped.

2

Nothing is deleted

A contradiction retires the old fact; it never removes it. The professional can always see what was believed, when, and what replaced it.

3

The escalation is code

The red-flag guard is 32 deterministic lines with a negation window. The model phrases the escalation; it never decides on one.

This is the slide that answers "how do we know it is not making the memory up".
