The professional pays, not the patient

Who buys

A physiotherapist, a midwife, a chronic-care nurse — anyone with a caseload of people who should be doing something at home between appointments.

How it is sold

Monthly subscription per professional, tiered by active patients. Synthflow starts at US$99 a month; a lower entry price aimed at Latin America is competitive.

Unit economics

Cents per call on AssemblyAI's streaming path — 333 free hours, then US$0.15 an hour — against a 15-minute call the professional has no time to make.

Market

Not "physiotherapists in Argentina": any health professional doing longitudinal follow-up. One engine, many verticals.
